Privacy Policy for Image Resizer

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Image Resizer is developed by Different Freelancer Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles user data and how third-party advertising services used by the app may process information.

1. Image Processing

Images selected by the user are processed locally on the user's device. Image Resizer does not upload selected images to the developer's servers, Google AdMob, or a cloud image-processing service. The developer does not receive or store the contents of images processed by the app.

2. File Access

The application only accesses images and folders that the user explicitly selects through the Android system file picker. This access is used to resize images and save resulting files to the user-selected destination.

3. Accounts

Image Resizer does not require users to create an account or sign in. Different Freelancer Studio does not maintain a server-side user account database for Image Resizer.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

Image Resizer uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner and interstitial advertisements. According to Google's Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure documentation, the SDK may automatically collect and share information such as the device's IP address, user interactions with the app and advertisements, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID where applicable. Google states that this information is used for purposes including advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

The Android advertising ID is subject to Android and Google privacy controls and may be reset or deleted by the user. Certain advertising configurations may limit transmission of advertising identifiers.

Information processed by Google through the advertising SDK is governed by Google's own terms and privacy practices. For more information, see Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure information.

5. Consent and Privacy Choices

Image Resizer integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to support consent and advertising privacy choices where required. Depending on applicable law, region, and Google's consent requirements, a consent message or privacy options may be presented before or while advertising services are used. The availability and content of these choices may vary by user and region.

6. Internet Access

The core image-resizing functionality is performed locally. Internet access is used for Google AdMob advertising, consent and related advertising services. User-selected image contents are not uploaded as part of the image-resizing process.

7. Data Collection and Sharing

Different Freelancer Studio does not sell user-selected images or maintain a developer-operated database containing those images. Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share advertising-related technical and interaction data as described above. Selected image contents are not intentionally provided to Google AdMob by Image Resizer.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Image Resizer does not maintain a remote database of user images or user accounts. Files created by the application remain in the location selected by the user and can be deleted by the user at any time. Advertising-related information processed by Google is subject to Google's own retention, deletion, and privacy practices.

9. Children's Privacy

Image Resizer does not knowingly collect or maintain user-selected images or account information on developer-operated servers. Third-party advertising services may process technical and advertising-related information as described in this policy. The application's advertising configuration and Google Play declarations are intended to be maintained consistently with the app's declared target audience and applicable platform requirements.

10. Security

User-selected images are processed locally on the device. Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

11. Third-Party Services

The application uses Google AdMob, the Google Mobile Ads SDK, and Google's User Messaging Platform for advertising and privacy-consent functionality. These services may change their data practices independently of Image Resizer. Users should review Google's privacy information for current details.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features, advertising setup, SDKs, legal requirements, or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page.

13. Contact

For privacy questions about Image Resizer, contact Different Freelancer Studio using the developer contact information provided on the application's Google Play Store listing.
