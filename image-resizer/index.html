Privacy Policy for Image Resizer

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Image Resizer is developed by Different Freelancer Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles user data and how the third-party advertising service used by the app may process information.

1. Image Processing

Images selected by the user are processed locally on the user's device. Image Resizer does not upload selected images to the developer's servers, Unity Ads, or a cloud image-processing service. The developer does not receive or store the contents of images processed by the app.

2. File Access

The application only accesses images and folders that the user explicitly selects through the Android system file picker. This access is used to resize images and save resulting files to the user-selected destination.

3. Accounts

Image Resizer does not require users to create an account or sign in. Different Freelancer Studio does not maintain a server-side user account database for Image Resizer.

4. Advertising and Unity Ads

Image Resizer uses Unity Ads, a service provided by Unity Technologies, to display banner and interstitial advertisements. The Unity Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information such as the device's IP address, device and operating system information, the Android advertising ID, app usage and ad interaction data, and diagnostic information. Unity states that this information is used for purposes including advertising, analytics, frequency capping, and fraud prevention.

The Android advertising ID is subject to Android and Google privacy controls and may be reset or deleted by the user. Certain advertising configurations may limit transmission of advertising identifiers.

Information processed by Unity through the advertising SDK is governed by Unity's own terms and privacy practices. For more information, see the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

5. Consent and Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law and region, Unity Ads may present privacy information or choices, such as an opt-out of personalized advertising, within or alongside advertisements. Users can also limit ad personalization through their device's advertising ID settings. The availability and content of these choices may vary by user and region.

6. Internet Access

The core image-resizing functionality is performed locally. Internet access is used for Unity Ads advertising and related services. User-selected image contents are not uploaded as part of the image-resizing process.

7. Data Collection and Sharing

Different Freelancer Studio does not sell user-selected images or maintain a developer-operated database containing those images. The Unity Ads SDK may collect and share advertising-related technical and interaction data as described above. Selected image contents are not intentionally provided to Unity Ads by Image Resizer.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Image Resizer does not maintain a remote database of user images or user accounts. Files created by the application remain in the location selected by the user and can be deleted by the user at any time. Advertising-related information processed by Unity is subject to Unity's own retention, deletion, and privacy practices.

9. Children's Privacy

Image Resizer is not directed primarily to children and does not knowingly collect or maintain user-selected images or account information on developer-operated servers. Third-party advertising services may process technical and advertising-related information as described in this policy. The application's advertising configuration and Google Play declarations are intended to be maintained consistently with the app's declared target audience and applicable platform requirements.

10. Security

User-selected images are processed locally on the device. Communication between the Unity Ads SDK and Unity's servers is handled by Unity using encrypted connections.

11. Third-Party Services

The application uses Unity Ads for advertising. This service may change its data practices independently of Image Resizer. Users should review Unity's privacy information for current details.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features, advertising setup, SDKs, legal requirements, or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page.

13. Contact

For privacy questions about Image Resizer, contact Different Freelancer Studio using the developer contact information provided on the application's Google Play Store listing.
